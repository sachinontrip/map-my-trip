✨ Type your trip

Write your trip in plain words — English or Hinglish. Each part becomes a scene.

What can I write?
  • Travel: "Delhi to Cairo by flight", "taxi to the pyramids", "Mumbai se Goa train", "walk to India Gate"
  • Countries: "show Egypt's neighbours", "highlight Nepal, Bhutan and Bangladesh", "Kerala aur Goa dikhao"
  • Close-up of a place: "visit the Taj Mahal", "explore Eiffel Tower"
  • Days: "Day 2: Cairo to Luxor by train"
  • Separate parts with commas, full stops, new lines, "then" or "phir".
